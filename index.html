THE WHY

Learn to fly.
Be ready.
Go where help is needed.

Ariana doesn't dream about flying only for the view from the cockpit. She dreams about what being a pilot could allow her to do for someone else.

Wildfires. Emergencies. Humanitarian needs. A place that's hard to reach. A moment nobody can predict. She wants to begin preparing now, so that someday she can be one of the people who can actually help.

Her goal is simple: learn now, build the skills responsibly, and someday be ready to fly when people need help.

THE DREAM WAS ALREADY THERE

Before there was a flight plan, there was curiosity.

These aren't campaign stock photos. They're Ariana's real family photographs — years of looking at airplanes, climbing into aviation exhibits, standing on ramps and watching aircraft through terminal windows.

Now that curiosity has a direction.

THE FLIGHT PLAN

A dream becomes a mission one step at a time.

12

Start Now

Explore aviation, fly with instructors, learn the fundamentals and build a real aviation education.

14–15

Build Knowledge

Ground school, glider opportunities, STEM, simulator work and scholarship preparation.

16

First Solo

Become eligible for an airplane student pilot certificate and work toward the unforgettable first solo.

17+

Earn the Wings

Private pilot, advanced ratings, professional experience and a lifetime of learning.

THE CONVERSATION

Where do we take off from here?

Ariana is still discovering exactly where aviation may take her. The purpose of this project is not to write the ending for her. It is to give her the opportunity to begin — to learn, meet mentors, experience aviation and find out how far she wants to go.
