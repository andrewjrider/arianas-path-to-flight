THE DREAM WAS ALREADY THERE

A girl who already loves aviation.

This isn't a new idea. The excitement, the curiosity and the smiles have been there for a long time.

Before there was a campaign, there were airport windows, aircraft exhibits and family days spent getting closer to airplanes. Now that curiosity has a direction.

This isn't the story of a pilot yet. It's the story of becoming one.

WHY FLY?

Learn to fly. Be ready.
Go where help is needed.

Ariana doesn't have to choose her entire future at twelve. The purpose is to give her a real beginning — instruction, aviation education, mentors and opportunities — and let each step tell us whether she wants the next one.

Wildfire aviation. Emergency response. Humanitarian work. Or a mission she hasn't discovered yet. The destination can change. The preparation still matters.

THE FLIGHT PLAN

A runway, not a deadline.

Start now. Build carefully. Let the dream earn its next step.

12

Start

Introductory flying, aviation education, mentors and exploration.

13

Build

Ground knowledge, simulator work, STEM and consistent exposure.

14–15

Explore

Glider opportunities, deeper ground school and scholarship preparation.

16

Solo

When ready and eligible, work toward the first airplane solo.

17+

Earn the wings

Private pilot certification, then decide what comes next.

18+

Find her mission

Advanced training and the long road toward service aviation if she chooses it.

FROM DAD

My job isn't to decide where Ariana lands.

I can't become a pilot for her. I can't know whether she'll want exactly the same thing at 16, 18 or 25. But I can take seriously what she's telling me at 12. I can find opportunities. I can put good people around her. I can help her start.

My job is to help make sure she has a runway.
